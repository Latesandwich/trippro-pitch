SOURCES: park visitors in fiscal year 2568 (2025), Department of National Parks via Nation Thailand, 12 Dec 2025. 56 million LINE users in Thailand, LINE Thailand, Aug 2026. 402,364 new motorcycles of more than 125cc sold in Thailand in 2567 (2024), down 16.4% on 2566: Krungsri Research, Industry Outlook 2025-2027: Motorcycle Industry, April 2025, page 8 (its band is "engine capacity in excess of 126 cc"). IF ASKED ABOUT 150cc: bikes between 126 and 149cc are rare in Thailand (the CBR150R and CB150R are 149cc), so this band is the 150cc class and up. The Honda PCX 160 alone sold 145,349 that year and the Click 160 70,023. Big bikes of 400cc and up were 15,468 registrations in 2566 (Thai Honda via Khaosod English).
